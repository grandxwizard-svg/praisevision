Our Story

Built by Players, for Players

PraiseJug's Ware was founded by competitive Fortnite players who were tired of guessing their way to improvement.

Our Mission

In 2022, a group of competitive Fortnite players recognized a gap in the market: there were plenty of "aim trainers" but nothing specifically engineered for the unique mechanics of Fortnite — drag editing, building combos, piece control, and high-sensitivity play.

PraiseJug's Ware was born from that frustration. We spent 18 months building, testing, and iterating with real competitive players before launching our first tool. Today, we're proud to offer the most comprehensive Fortnite-specific training suite on the market.
